About NotesApp

NotesApp is a modern, secure, and easy-to-use platform to store and manage your personal notes. With professional UI, cloud-based storage, and strong security, NotesApp ensures your notes stay organized, private, and accessible anytime.

✨ Key Features

  • Create, update, delete, and search notes easily
  • Fully private notes — each user has separate workspace
  • Secure login with hashed passwords
  • Professional SaaS-style UI
  • Password reset via email
  • Fast, responsive, and mobile-friendly design
